PDFFill Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Applies to the PDFFill app for Android (de.bdki.pdffill)

In short: PDFFill does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your documents and photos stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Who we are

PDFFill is published on Google Play by Dr. Hua Bi. For any question about this policy or about your privacy, write to support.bdki@gmail.com.

What the app does with your data

PDFFill lets you open a PDF or scan a paper document, add a handwritten signature or typed text, and save the result as a new file. All of this happens on your device:

  • Documents you open are read from the file you pick, only to show and edit them. The original file is never changed.
  • Signatures and text you add exist only in the app's memory until you save, and then only inside the new PDF.
  • Saved and shared files go where you choose: a folder you pick, or the app you pick in Android's share sheet. To hand a file to that app, PDFFill writes a temporary copy to its private cache, which Android can clear at any time and which is deleted when you uninstall the app.

PDFFill has no servers. It does not send your documents, photos, or any information about you or your device over the internet, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software.

Camera

PDFFill asks for camera permission only so you can scan a paper document. The camera is active only while the scan screen is open. Each photo is turned into a page of a PDF on your device and is not uploaded or kept anywhere else. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in Android's settings at any time; everything except scanning keeps working.

Google Play and Android

Google provides the Play Store and Android, and processes data when you download, install, and update apps, under Google's Privacy Policy. PDFFill receives none of that data. PDFFill keeps no documents in its own storage, so a device backup contains nothing of yours from the app.

Children

PDFFill is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because PDFFill does not collect or store personal data, we hold nothing about you that could be accessed, corrected, exported, or deleted. If you have a question or a request under data-protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that handles data differently, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date before that version of the app is released.
